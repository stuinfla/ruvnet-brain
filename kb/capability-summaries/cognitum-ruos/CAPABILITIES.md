# Cognitum ruOS capabilities

Cognitum ruOS is an agentic operating system for AI workstations. It is designed
to observe workstation conditions, reason about what needs attention, and take
appropriate actions with less manual intervention.

Its documented capabilities include:

- Workstation health monitoring and recovery from service failures.
- Adapting performance profiles to workload and user context.
- Local AI assistance and persistent knowledge for workstation tasks.
- Learning from prior sessions and evaluating knowledge quality.
- Backup, recovery, and software updates.
- Voice interaction and optional sensing with user consent.
- Security checks around AI interactions.

It is relevant when a user wants a workstation that helps maintain itself and
supports ongoing AI-assisted work. These are documented capabilities, not a claim
that ruOS is installed, provisioned, or independently benchmarked on this machine.

This Brain entry intentionally covers capabilities and use cases only. Proprietary
source code, internal architecture, algorithms, configuration, and implementation
instructions are outside its published knowledge scope.
