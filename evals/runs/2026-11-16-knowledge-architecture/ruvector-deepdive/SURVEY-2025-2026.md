# What shipped in 2025-2026 that bears on storing and retrieving this corpus

Two parts: what rUv shipped (dated from git, ADR date fields, changelogs, gist dates and the npm registry),
and what the wider field shipped (dated from the sources' own pages). Full tables with every row's source:
`raw/I-ruv-2025-2026-timeline.md` (266 lines) and `raw/H-external-2025-2026-survey.md` (225 lines).

How far to trust it. Part 1 dates were opened by a reader in git, ADRs and the registry; I re-checked the npm
versions and the items I cite elsewhere. Part 2: the reader opened five pages; the rest are search-result
summaries, and the raw file tags each row. I fetched two myself (marked **F**). Vendor scores are
self-reported and not comparable with each other; the sources say so.

## What changed between 2024 and late 2026 that matters here

1. Memory for agents moved from "fetch the nearest text" to **rebuilding an answer by walking a cue graph**,
   in a peer-reviewed paper with real benchmarks (June 2026), and rUv shipped an implementation three weeks
   later.
2. **Vague, reasoning-style questions became a measured problem** (BRIGHT): the best 2024-style retrievers
   lose two thirds of their score on them. That is the amateur's question, with a number on it.
3. **A model rewriting the question helps** (24.4 to 29.9), and in this product the calling model is already
   there.
4. **Choosing the right tool is recognised as its own retrieval problem** (ToolRet 33.8; RAG-MCP 13.6% to
   43.1%), and the working answer is to retrieve over descriptions of what a tool is for.
5. Coding agents split: one drops the index for search by grep, one measures +12.5% from adding semantic
   search to grep. Both keep the keyword path. This matches the lead's "lexical backbone".
6. **Optimising the harness, judged on held-out data**, is now an established method (GEPA, Meta-Harness),
   and rUv ships a tool for it.
7. Abstention has formal tools (conformal methods) rather than a hand-set threshold.
8. Small re-rankers (17 M to 70 M parameters) with measured CPU speed exist under permissive licences.
9. Memory stores can branch and roll back cheaply (RVF, agenticow, 2026).
10. Typed graphs with vectors bound to node types exist in rUv's stack (June 2026).
11. Memory leaderboard numbers are self-reported and disputed; none transfers to this corpus without testing.
