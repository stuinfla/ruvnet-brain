What “proactive” means here

Six levels, L0 to L5, and an honest marker of which one this build has actually reached. at level 2 of 5 ›

“Proactive” is the claim this whole product rests on, so here is exactly what it commits us to — and where it currently stops.

  1. L0 · ReactiveAnswers when you ask. A question gets a grounded, cited answer.
  2. L1 · ObservantNotices and displays the state of your machine — the capabilities card above, in this section.
  3. L2 · AdvocatingFinds a dormant capability, proposes the fix, applies it once you consent, and can undo it. Every suggestion resolves to one working executor and one real undo. This build is here.
  4. L3 · ContextualSpeaks up inside your working session, at the moment it matters, without you opening this page.
  5. L4 · AnticipatoryWorks out what you are trying to do and names the better path before you commit to the worse one.
  6. L5 · CompoundingChecks whether its own advice was right, and promotes what held up so every project inherits it.

L3, L4 and L5 are not built, and this page will not imply otherwise — each level is a falsifiable test, not a mood, and the top three do not pass yet. The capabilities card in this section is L1 doing its job; anything with an undo behind it is L2. Full definitions and the tests each level has to pass: docs/adr/0028.

The one thing on this list you can actually set is the volume on advocacy itself: the “How much it volunteers” dial, in the Settings card in this same section. Everything above is a description of the software, not a switch.
