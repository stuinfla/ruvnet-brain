TriSmart gives an important architectural decision a small, independent review board. It connects the subscription CLIs you already use, lets them disagree productively, and only hands a design to implementation after the selected models accept the same answer.

independent proposals → one verified design

The problem it solves

Important architecture decisions are easy to get wrong when one model makes one confident guess. TriSmart gives the decision a small review board: independent ideas, direct challenge, one reconciled design, and a hard stop when a critical disagreement remains.
